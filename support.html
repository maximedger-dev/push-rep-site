Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?
Open the Coach tab, then tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose PushRep. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account and data?
In the app go to Settings, then Delete account. It permanently removes your login, your cloud copy and everything stored on your phone.

The AI Coach is not answering.
Check your connection and your credits (the diamond at the top of the Coach tab). If it keeps failing, email us with roughly what time it happened.

My notifications are not arriving.
Check Settings, Notifications, PushRep on your iPhone, and the Notifications card in PushRep's own Settings.
