Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

PushRep ("the app") is provided by Maxim Edger ("we"). Contact: maxim.edger03@gmail.com. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

What stays on your device

Your workouts, bodyweight entries, meals, settings and coach chat history are stored on your device. If you do not log in, we cannot see this data unless you use an AI feature (see below). If you log in, a copy is also saved to our server (see "Your PushRep account and cloud backup").

Barcode scanner

The scanner uses your camera only to read the number of a food barcode. It does not take, save or send pictures. The camera is used only while the scanner is open, and only if you allow it in iPhone Settings.

To find the product, the app sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a free, open food database run by a third party, and receives the product name and nutrition back. Like any internet request, this shows them your device's IP address. No account details or other data from the app are sent. Open Food Facts handles that request under its own privacy policy. Products you scan and foods you enter by hand are saved on your device only.

Pump pictures and progress pictures (optional)

After a workout you can add up to three pump pics, taken with the camera or chosen from your photo library, you can add progress pictures, and after a cardio session you can add one photo for that session. PushRep only sees the photos you pick. The camera and photo library are used only when you tap the button, and only if you allow it in iPhone Settings.

Your pump and progress pictures are shrunk and kept on your device. We never receive them: they are not uploaded, not in the cloud copy and not sent to the AI. (A photo of a meal is different: see "Meal photos" below.) PushRep can turn a workout and your pump pic into a picture card with the PushRep name on it, and can turn a cardio session and its photo into a picture card with the PushRep name, the outline of your route (drawn on your phone from the route kept on your phone), and your time, distance, average pace and calories. Those cards are only made and sent when you press Share, and they go wherever you choose in the iPhone share sheet (for example Instagram, Messages or Save Image), under that app's own rules. When you choose a progress photo from your photo library, PushRep reads the day the photo was taken, from the photo's own information, to suggest that day for the picture. Nothing else from the photo's information is kept, and the day is not sent anywhere. You can delete a picture at any time from the app, and deleting your account removes all of them from your phone.

Meal photos (optional, only if you choose)

In Nutrition, Ask AI, you can take or choose a photo of a meal, and type what is in it, so the AI can estimate its calories and protein, carbs and fat. Only when you press Estimate, and only after the app has asked your permission to send the photo, the photo (shrunk to about 1000 pixels wide) and your description are sent to our server, which passes them to our AI provider, Anthropic, to read the meal. The photo is used only to produce the answer. We do not keep it on our server, do not add it to your cloud copy, and do not use it for advertising or sell it. The photo stays on your phone only until you add the food or leave the screen. Anthropic handles it under its commercial terms and privacy policy (see "What we receive when you use an AI feature").

Calorie calculator

The calculator (offered when you first set up the app, and any time in Settings) uses the age, height, weight, sex and activity level you type to estimate your daily calories. They are not sent to the AI provider. If you have an account they are part of your settings in the cloud copy described below; if you do not, they stay on your device. The result is an estimate and not medical advice.

Your name

First-time setup asks what to call you. It is used to greet you in the app, is kept on your device (and in your cloud copy, as part of your settings, if you log in), and you can change it in Settings. It is not part of the summary sent to the AI provider.

Location (optional, only while you track cardio)

If you start a run, ride, walk or other outdoor cardio session with "Track with GPS" on (it is on by default and can be switched off in Settings or for one session), PushRep asks the iPhone for permission to use your precise location. It uses your location only during a session you started, to work out the distance, your pace for every kilometre (or mile), your climb, and to draw your route and where you are on a map. It does not use your location at any other time, does not follow you between sessions, and does not use it for advertising or marketing.

The first time you ever start a tracked session, PushRep asks the iPhone for "While Using the App" and then, straight after, for "Always", so the questions come together at the start and not again in the middle of a run (you can answer either way; with only "While Using" the app still tracks while it is open, and it can ask again from the session screen). Always lets tracking carry on with the screen locked, and it is used only while a tracked session is running (the iPhone shows its blue location indicator), and tracking stops when you finish or discard the session. You can change or withdraw the permission at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

What is kept: the distance, the pace for each kilometre, and a thinned-out route (at most about 160 points) are saved with the session on your device only. They are not sent to our server, to the AI provider, to RevenueCat, to Apple Health or to other users, and they are not part of the optional cloud backup. To show the names of the start and the end of a route, the app asks the iPhone's own address lookup (Apple's service) for the name of a place; that request goes from your phone to Apple and is not seen by us. The map itself is Apple Maps (the iPhone's own map): to draw it, the iPhone loads the map pictures for the area you are looking at from Apple, as any map app does. Your route is drawn on top of it on your phone; PushRep does not send the route to Apple or anyone else.

Apple Health (optional)

If you connect Apple Health, PushRep asks Apple for your permission, separately for each kind of data. During first-time setup the app asks for everything it uses in one go, and Apple's own screen lets you switch each kind on or off. With your permission it reads your body weight, your height, your date of birth and your sex (once, during first-time setup, to fill in the calorie calculator, in boxes you can change; your date of birth is turned into an age and is not kept), and the calories of workouts recorded by your Apple Watch or other apps. It can also save the workouts you log in PushRep (strength sessions, and walks, runs, rides and other cardio, with the distance when the phone measured one) and weights you enter, into Apple Health. It does not save calorie figures.

Information from Apple Health stays on your device. We do not send it to our server, to the AI provider, to RevenueCat or to other users, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing, or sell it. Weights that came from Apple Health are left out of the summary the AI coach receives. If you accept the height or weight that PushRep filled in from Apple Health during setup, it is then kept as a number you entered yourself, like one you typed (and, if you have an account, it is included in your cloud copy). You can disconnect in Settings, and change or remove PushRep's access at any time in the Health app (your picture, Apps, PushRep).

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules notifications on your device: one to tell you your rest is over when you use the rest timer, and, each one switchable in Settings, Notifications: three a day at about 10:30, 12:30 and 18:30 reminding you to log your meals; one every day at about 05:00 reminding you to weigh yourself; one every day at about 09:30 reminding you to log yesterday's calories burnt; one every day at about 12:10 asking "Working out today?"; and one every day at about 22:00 asking you to log the calories you burnt that day. They only invite you to open the app. They are created on your device, are not sent from a server and carry no personal data.

The lock screen card and sounds

While a workout, a rest or a tracked run is going, PushRep can show a card on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island (an iPhone "Live Activity"). It shows the time, the sets you have done, the name of the workout or the next exercise, the rest countdown, and for a run the time, distance and pace. It is made and updated on your device only: it is not sent to our server or anyone else, and it does not use push notifications. Anyone who can see your lock screen can see the card, so you can switch it off in the app (Settings, Training, Lock screen card), swipe it away, or turn Live Activities off for PushRep in the iPhone's Settings. It ends when the workout or session ends.

PushRep plays two short sounds that are part of the app (one on the start-up screen and one when you press the logo). They respect the iPhone's silent switch, they can be switched off in Settings (Sound effects), and the app does not record sound or use the microphone.

What we receive when you use an AI feature

When you use the AI coach, AI analysis or "Log with AI", and only after you agree in the app, the app sends to our server:

  • your question or food description, or (only when you choose and agree) the photo of a meal
  • a short summary of recent workouts, bodyweight entries you typed yourself (not weights that came from Apple Health), nutrition targets and daily food totals
  • a random identifier used to check your subscription and apply usage limits (or your PushRep account id, if you are logged in)

Our server passes this to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate the reply. For some food questions the AI may search the web for ideas, and it then shows you the pages it used as links. The search is made by Anthropic's service, from your question; nothing else from your data is sent to those websites by us. We do not use this data for advertising and we do not sell it. Anthropic handles the content under its commercial terms and privacy policy: it is used to produce your answer and is not used to train its models without permission, and Anthropic may keep content for a limited period as its own policies allow (for example where content is flagged for safety review). We keep only a daily count of AI requests, and a count of your coach credits (how many are left this month and how many you have bought), per identifier, to apply usage limits. We do not keep your questions or the AI's answers on our server; your chat history stays on your device.

Your PushRep account and cloud backup (optional)

You can use PushRep without an account. If you choose "Sign in with Apple" or "Sign in with Google" on the first screen (or later in Settings), we create an account for you so your data is saved in the cloud and can be put back on a new phone. We store on our server (hosted by Supabase):

  • your account details from Apple or Google: a user identifier, your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real one) and, if you share it, your name. We never see your Apple or Google password
  • a copy of your workouts, meals, bodyweight entries, custom exercises and settings, and when and from which kind of device it was last saved. It is updated a few seconds after you change something and when you close the app, and only you can read it. Weights and calories that came from Apple Health, pump pictures, progress pictures, routes, your coach chat history and any meal photo are not part of this copy
  • if you use an affiliate code, that you did, so it works on every phone you log in on

We use this only to run the app: to log you in and to give you your data back. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it. When you log out, a last copy is saved and the data is removed from your phone; logging in again brings it back.

You can delete your account in the app: Settings, Delete account. This permanently removes your login, your cloud copy, any pictures left from an older version of the app, your affiliate code, AI usage counts and coach credit count from our server, and every workout, meal, weight, picture and setting from your phone. It cannot be undone. It does not cancel an App Store subscription, which you manage in your Apple Account settings. Our hosting provider may keep routine backups of its systems for a short period after deletion, after which they are overwritten.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an app user identifier (random, or your PushRep account id if you are logged in, so your subscription follows you to a new phone) and purchase information. Apple does not share your payment details with us. Coach credits you buy are one-off purchases recorded against that identifier.

Who processes data for us

  • Apple (purchases, App Store, maps, and Sign in with Apple)
  • RevenueCat (subscription status)
  • Supabase (hosting of our server function, the database, your PushRep account login and cloud backup)
  • Google (only to log you in, if you choose it)
  • Anthropic (AI replies, and web search for some food questions)
  • Open Food Facts (looking up the barcode numbers you scan)

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens they rely on recognised safeguards for international transfers. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Why we use your data (UK GDPR)

To give you the app and the features you ask for, including your account, backup and subscription (performing our contract with you); to send your data to the AI provider when you agree in the app (your consent, which you can withdraw by not using the feature or by deleting your account); and to keep the app secure and working (our legitimate interests). We keep data on our server for as long as you have an account, and delete it when you delete the account.

Your choices and rights

You can export or delete all data held on your device from Settings, and delete your PushRep account and everything stored for it from Settings, Delete account. If you are in the UK or EU you have rights to access, correct, delete and restrict use of personal data we hold, and to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). Email maxim.edger03@gmail.com to use these rights. Deleting the app removes the on-device data (an account and its cloud copy stay until you delete the account in the app or ask us).

Medical disclaimer
